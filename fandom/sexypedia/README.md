# sexypedia

After Fandom decided to close the famous encyclopedia about [Tumblr sexymen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumblr_Sexyman), their wiki moved to [Miraheze](https://sexypedia.miraheze.org/). However, they were left without thousands of images.

This Node.js script read the content page dump generated upon closure of the Fandom wiki and downloaded the images from Fandom servers, so all images from the wiki were preserved. However, the contributors decided not to use them in the end.
